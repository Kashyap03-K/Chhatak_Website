What is Chhatak?

Chhatak is a coastal Indian brand crafting a ready-to-eat Bombil fish snack — dried Bombay Duck seasoned in traditional Diu spices, then baked and packed for a crunch that goes with drinks, meals, movies and travel. Wild-caught. 21g+ protein per 100g. No preservatives. Made in Diu.
